  • Mon, Sept 14, "A Graphical Tour of the Cosmos" with Dr. Ed Friedman for the Denver Astronomical Society. 7:00 - 9:00 pm. 254 Knox Ct, Denver, 80219.
    This presentation features animated graphical tools - built on real solutions to Einstein’s equations of gravity - that let us explore the universe visually, without requiring you to work through the math yourself. Using real data from ground and space telescopes, including Planck, DESI, and JWST, we'll explore the cosmos’s historical rate of expansion, its geometry, the velocity of distant galaxies, its age, and its future. We'll see how measurements of things we can observe reveal properties we can't observe directly, and how independent methods - the cosmic microwave background, baryon acoustic oscillations, and supernova surveys - converge on a consistent picture. We'll dig into the current central puzzle of the Hubble tension, recent controversies in dark energy measurements, and JWST's surprising discoveries about the early universe. This talk is a window into the actual techniques professional astronomers use to build our current understanding of the cosmos.
